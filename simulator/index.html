How the impact is worked out. Each dollar earns the return in Exhibit 3 of the case: field force 1.8, digital 3.1, medical events 1.5, patient support 2.4. Field force adds nothing above 55% of a market's budget, and digital earns 1.5 above 30%. The impact is the difference against the current mix. If field spending above 55% still earned 1.8, the recommended mix would add USD 7.3m and not USD 10.2m. Each market keeps its own budget, so the total is always USD 60m. The case allows no more than 25% of a market's budget to move in a year.
